# Dynamical program figure captions

These captions describe the predetermined figures in `results/dynamical_generalization/dg_v1_20261103/figures/`. The corresponding long-format tables, source code and per-stage output hashes are indexed in the result manifests. Cohorts are never combined for inference.

**E1 — Dynamical accessibility.** Upper panels: one point per seed and primary probe family, showing natural sequence CE against output-relevant one-step midpoint closure defect, separately for canonical and constructive cohorts. Novel directions are averaged within seed. Lower left: canonical High task-space error after one midpoint placement, averaged over available injection positions and paired Novel directions within seed before the across-seed mean; lag zero is an exogenous placement. At lag l, 7−l positions contribute. Lower right: paired C2−C0 and C3−C1 changes in mean closure defect and natural CE, retaining one point per seed and probe family. Neither scatter is a mediation analysis. Source: `e1_seed_summary.csv`, `e1_single_patch_replay.csv`, `e1_seed_effects.csv`.

**E2 — Independent cue-coordinate grid.** Columns show canonical High, canonical Low, and separate constructive C0–C3 conditions. Top row: mean seven-transition natural CE in nats. Bottom row: mean output-relevant one-step closure defect in absolute centered-logit units. Horizontal axis is alpha=x−y; vertical axis is beta=x+y. Each panel uses its own displayed color scale. The 35 grid locations use the declared decimal coordinate values; exact radical Novel cues are retained separately as anchors in the source tables. All raw third-channel values are zero. Norm, angular distance and distance to the familiar convex segment are distinct audit columns; a beta coordinate alone is not psychological familiarity. Source: `e2_grid_seed.csv`; comparisons and uncertainty: `e2_matched_geometry_controls.csv` and `e2_controls_bootstrap.csv`.

**E3 pilot — Familiar-only feasibility.** Left: one point for each of 48 candidate settings, with the smaller strong-minus-weak separation gap and the smaller closure-arm relative improvement across the two relevant factorial comparisons. The vertical and horizontal lines mark the prospectively fixed 0.25-unit separation and 1% closure criteria. Color indicates whether familiar competence met its separate prespecified bounds. Right: number of candidates passing each criterion and their conjunction. This is calibration on five reserved seeds, not confirmatory probe evidence. Source: `e3_pilot_candidate_audit.csv`.

**E3 factorial, when eligible.** Familiar competence, realized hidden separation, fixed-H neutral closure, and protected natural sequence CE are displayed for all four arms. Individual dots represent trained seeds. The randomized treatments act on separation pressure and closure loss; familiar CE imbalance and unsuccessful target engagement limit causal interpretation. Source: `e3_familiar_seed.csv`, `e3_seed_summary.csv`.

**E4 — Fixed-sequence replication grid.** Each panel retains a distinct registered sequence and the same frozen factorial design. If the E3 familiar-only pilot is infeasible, every panel is explicitly design-only and no outcome is plotted. Otherwise, panels show per-sequence closure × separation CE interactions and paired-seed percentile intervals. Three fixed sequences do not establish universality across sequences or architectures. Source: `e4_sequence_validation.csv` and, when estimable, `e4_interaction_bootstrap.csv`.
