# Paper analysis contract

The machine-readable contract is [`configs/paper_freeze_v2.json`](../configs/paper_freeze_v2.json). Its content hash, source-code hash, cue-registry digest, checkpoint paths and SHA256 digests, supporting table hashes, cohorts, seed list, matching rule, and training prohibition are validated before evaluation. Outputs carry these identifiers and are written to a new versioned directory only. The frozen registry is [`configs/novelty_registry_v1.json`](../configs/novelty_registry_v1.json); its geometric acceptance tests preceded the first new-cue evaluation.

Primary behavior is raw seven-transition sequence CE in nats. Familiar reference CE is the equal mean of A and B within seed. Primary geometry is absolute Euclidean distance between centered logits and the A/B midpoint; normalized residual uses the transition-specific A/B separation plus epsilon. Hidden geometry is secondary. Natural, repeated, acute, local-branch, and takeover measurements have separate `analysis_kind` and intervention labels. The exported transition tables preserve probe, seed, transition, position, continuation, and lag where relevant. Their seed tables average only the registered within-seed dimensions. The plotted panel tables and `claim_source_map.csv` state the exact source, filter, and estimator.

A1 compares High/Low natural behavior and differentiation. A2 applies the familiar-only 0.05-nat A/B matching rule at High update 170 and reports support even when the behavioral estimate is unavailable. A3 tests familiar midpoint placement against natural and control placements. A4 tests maintenance after placement under D0/D1/D2. A5 tests physical-cue generation and takeover. A6 tests C0–C3 natural behavior. A7 pairs C3−C0 geometry with C3−C0 CE. A8 audits the contrast projection and deterministic aliases. All four main figures and specified supplements are produced regardless of the signs or intervals of their effects.

Technical corrections after the first evaluation are versioned rather than overwriting it. The current `r2` freeze changes only schematic text placement in Figures 2 and 4; the first output remains intact. No statistical definition, evaluator, checkpoint, or cue changed. Both contracts' source hashes are preserved in their respective manifests, and the current JSON points to `r2`.
